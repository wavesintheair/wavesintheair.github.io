Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

WavesInTheAir uses a private, internal application ("WavesInTheAir Publisher") to publish its own videos to its own official accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.

1. Data accessed

The application only accesses WavesInTheAir's own accounts, with the account owner's authorization, to upload and schedule videos, read statistics about those videos, and read and reply to comments on them.

2. Third-party data

We do not collect, sell or share personal data. Public comments are read only to be answered on the same platform.

3. Storage

Access tokens are stored privately on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time in the settings of each platform (Google account permissions, Instagram "Apps and websites", TikTok "Manage app permissions").

4. Platform policies

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This application uses YouTube API Services; the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy also apply, as do the Meta Platform Terms and the TikTok Developer Terms.

5. Data deletion

To request deletion of any data, contact us through the Instagram account @wavesintheair. Revoking access in the platform settings immediately stops all access.
